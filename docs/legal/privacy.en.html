Privacy Policy

Last updated · June 6, 2026

LugAI ("the App") values your privacy and processes only the minimum information needed to provide the service. This policy explains what information the App processes and how.

1. Information we process

• Photos: Photos you take or pick to analyze your luggage are processed. They are sent temporarily to an AI server for analysis and are not stored on the server afterward. • Trips/verdicts: Trips and item verdicts you save are stored only on your device (local storage). • The App does not collect identifying information such as accounts, names, emails, or precise location.

2. Purpose

Photos are used solely to identify items and provide per-country baggage rule verdicts.

3. Third-party processing

Photo analysis is performed via Anthropic's Claude AI. Photos are sent only for analysis and are never sold or shared for advertising or marketing.

4. Retention & deletion

Trip records are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the App. Photos sent for analysis are discarded immediately after processing and are not retained.

5. Permissions

• Camera: used to photograph and analyze your luggage. • Photos (album): used to pick existing photos for analysis. You can change permissions anytime in device settings.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Your rights

You can delete trip records stored on your device directly within the App. For other inquiries, contact us below.

8. Limits of verdicts

Verdicts are reference information only. Actual admissibility depends on airline and destination-country rules; final confirmation is the user's responsibility.

9. Changes

This policy may change; significant changes will be announced through the App.

10. Contact

Privacy inquiries: support@lugai.app
